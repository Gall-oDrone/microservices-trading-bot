# Trading risk alerts: runbook

Alerts: [`monitoring/prometheus/rules/trading-risk-alerts.yml`](../../monitoring/prometheus/rules/trading-risk-alerts.yml)
(k8s copy generated by `scripts/sync-prometheus-rules.sh`). Dashboard: **Trading Risk Operations**
(`monitoring/grafana/dashboards/domain/trading-risk-operations.json`). Design: plan §6.4.4 in
[`FRONTEND-UI-PLAN-2026-10-03.md`](../frontend/FRONTEND-UI-PLAN-2026-10-03.md).

**Severity:** `critical` = page now, act within minutes; `warning` = review the same trading day and
record the outcome; `info` = record only. **Team:** `risk` (limits, halts) or `trading` (execution,
reconciliation).

**Golden rules**
- When in doubt, halt. HALT ALL on the Risk page (or write the halt file by hand) stops the
  daily-executor, trading-engine and order-management. Resuming needs a reason and is audited.
- **In k8s**, the engines read the ConfigMap `trading-halt` instead (plan §6.4.5). Halt the
  cluster with `scripts/k8s-halt.sh -n <ns> halt --reason "..." --confirm <ns>`; it applies the
  change, nudges the pods and waits until every trading-engine and order-management pod sees it
  (`status` shows each pod's view). The Risk page's HALT ALL does not reach the cluster yet.
- Never raise a limit to clear an alert during the session. Limit changes go through a new
  `TRADING_RISK_POLICY` file, reviewed, with a new `version`.
- Record every breach attempt and every halt: who, what, when, why, outcome.

## Kill switch

### TradingKillSwitchEngaged
*warning.* `trading_halt_active == 1`: a halt file is halted (or invalid).
1. Expected after HALT ALL: confirm on the Risk page who halted, when and why (ui-api audit log).
   In k8s: `scripts/k8s-halt.sh -n <ns> status` and the ConfigMap annotations
   (`trading-halt/last-by`, `last-at`), plus the operator's `~/.trading-ops/k8s-halt-audit.jsonl`.
2. Not expected: read the halt files (`TRADING_HALT_FILES`), find who wrote them, then decide
   whether to resume from the UI (in k8s: `k8s-halt.sh resume --reason ... --confirm <ns>`).
3. Halted engines keep running and keep reporting; no order leaves until resumed.

### TradingHaltFileInvalid
*critical.* A halt file cannot be read or parsed (bad JSON, unknown field, halted without
reason/by/at). Every order is blocked, but nobody chose this halt.
1. The engine log names the file and the error at start-up and on each blocked order.
2. Fix the file with the UI (halt/resume writes a valid file) or remove it if the ledger is retired.
   In k8s someone hand-edited the ConfigMap: rewrite it with `k8s-halt.sh halt` or `resume`.
3. Do not delete a halted file to "unblock": resume from the UI so the action is audited.

### TradingKillSwitchUnreachable
*critical.* A live engine (`trading_engine_dry_run == 0`) watches no halt files, so HALT ALL cannot
stop it.
1. Set `TRADING_HALT_FILES` to the ledger halt files (the stage ledger's `risk-state.json`) and
   restart, or stop the engine until it is set.
2. In k8s the base manifests mount ConfigMap `trading-halt` at `/etc/trading-halt` and set
   `TRADING_HALT_FILES=/etc/trading-halt/risk-state.json`; an overlay or a manual edit removed it.
   CI (`check-k8s-halt-wiring.py`) fails on any overlay that drops it.

### TradingHaltWatcherStale
*warning.* The 10 s halt-file watcher stopped updating. Orders are still checked against the files
on every signal, but the dashboard's kill-switch tile is stale. Check the engine is running and not
stuck (goroutine dump, restart).

## Policy

### TradingRiskPolicyVersionDrift
*warning.* Replicas report different `trading_risk_policy_info{version}`. Roll out one policy file
and restart the odd replica. Until then, assume the loosest policy is live.

### TradingRiskStateUnknown
*critical.* The gate failed closed: order-management's exposure endpoint
(`/api/v1/risk/exposure`) did not answer, or a halt file is unreadable. Orders are blocked.
1. Check order-management health and `ORDER_MANAGEMENT_URL`.
2. Check `TradingHaltFileInvalid`.
3. If the outage lasts, halt explicitly so the state is unambiguous.

## Limits

### TradingLimitBreachAttempt
*warning.* An order was blocked by a hard limit (`max_order_btc`, `max_order_notional`,
`max_position_btc`, `max_price_deviation_bps`, `invalid_order`). Nothing was sent.
1. Find the strategy and signal in the engine log (`blocked by risk policy (<version>)`).
2. Was it a bug (wrong size, bad price feed) or the strategy outgrowing the limit? Record it.
3. Repeated attempts from one strategy: stop that strategy (Strategies page) until reviewed.

### TradingOrderLimitUtilizationHigh
*warning.* p95 of orders is above 90 % of a per-order limit over 1 h: the next size increase or a
price move will start breaching. Size down, or propose a reviewed limit change.

### TradingSessionLimitWarning / TradingSessionLimitBreached
*warning at 80 %, critical at 100 %* of `max_daily_loss` (quote currency) or `max_drawdown_pct`.
- At 80 %: tell the strategy owner; consider reducing size or stopping new entries.
- At 100 %: new orders are blocked for the session. **Open positions are not flattened
  automatically**, and the engine's session gate blocks sells as well as buys: decide explicitly
  whether to hold, or exit by hand on the exchange (recorded). Do not raise the limit intraday.

## Execution quality

### TradingPriceDeviationHigh
*warning.* Orders are priced far from the touch mid (p95 > 100 bps over 30 min): wide spread, stale
signal or an aggressive limit offset. Compare with `TradingSignalStale` and the book's spread.

### TradingSignalStale
*warning.* Signals are > 30 s old (p95) when checked: Kafka lag (`KafkaConsumerLagHigh`), a slow
consumer, or clock skew between the strategy host and the engine (timestamps are Unix seconds).

### TradingTickToTradeSlow
*warning.* Signal-to-exchange-ack p99 > 10 s. If `order_execution_duration_seconds` is also high the
exchange is slow; otherwise the delay is in the pipeline (see `TradingSignalStale`).

## Order management

### OrderExchangeSyncStale
*critical.* Order status has not been reconciled with Bitso for > 5 min. Open orders and fills in the
OMS may not match the exchange (reconciliation break). Check Bitso API health and the sync job's
errors (`bitso_sync_errors_total`). Halt if the break persists while orders are open.

### OrderFillDiscoveryStale
*critical.* Fills have not been polled for > 5 min, so positions, P&L and the session limits may be
stale. Same checks as above.

### OrderRejectRateHigh
*warning.* > 20 % of orders rejected by order-management over 15 min. Break down
`orders_rejected_total` by `reason` and `risk_violations_total` by `violation_type`.

### OrderFillRatioLow
*warning.* < 50 % of orders filled over 1 h. Limit prices too passive or liquidity thin; realized
costs and the forward test assume fills.

### OrderSharedPolicyBlockAtOMS
*warning.* order-management blocked an order on the shared policy or a halt (`shared_policy:<rule>`).
The engine gate should have stopped it first: check the engine's `TRADING_RISK_POLICY` /
`TRADING_HALT_FILES` match order-management's, and look for orders arriving by another path.
